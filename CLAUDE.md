# Midnite Systems Website — Editorial Rules

## Copy style

**No em dashes anywhere in site copy.** Use commas, colons, periods, or rewrite the sentence. This applies to all visible text: headings, subheadings, body copy, card descriptions, metadata, CTA copy, footer.

Reason: user's explicit preference. Em dashes read as AI-slop to this audience.

How to apply: when drafting or editing any user-facing copy in `app/`, scrub em dashes. En dashes are fine in contexts where they're numerically correct (e.g. "$1M–$50M"), but default to plain hyphens or commas elsewhere.

## Audience (internal context, not copy)

Ideal target: operating businesses with 5+ employees and $1M–$50M ARR. Do not mention "SMB," "small business," or the revenue range explicitly in copy. Positioning terms that work: "growing businesses," "operating businesses," "modern teams."

## Git

**Do not add `Co-Authored-By: Claude ...` trailers to commits in this repo.** User explicitly declined Claude coauthorship.
